About me

read first

I am a Telecommunications Infrastructure Engineer specialising in Artificial Intelligence Engineering within the governmental sector in Qatar, and I hold a Bachelor of Science in Electrical Engineering from Texas A&M University (2023).

I came to artificial intelligence through engineering practice rather than theory: a degree in electrical engineering, then telecommunications infrastructure, where machine learning tends to arrive already packaged — a set of tools to deploy, integrate and keep running, rather than one to interrogate.

I am seeking the theoretical grounding that practice does not supply: how intelligent systems represent knowledge, derive decisions, and fail, and what constitutes a defensible basis for evaluating an architecture rather than deferring to its reputation.

What follows is evidence, not summary. Each module page links to the work itself — the code, the report, the recording — so you can check the claim against the artefact.
