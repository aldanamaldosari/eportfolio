KQML / KIF Agent Playground

You act as Alice, the procurement agent. Compose ask-one queries and send them to Bob, the warehouse agent. Bob answers with tell when he holds the fact — and sorry when the ontology or his knowledge base rejects the query.

Try weight-kg to see Bob reject a function the ontology doesn't define, or hdmi-slots on the soundbar for a missing fact.

Type anything — e.g. how many TVs do you have? — and Bob replies sorry: he only parses (function instance ?variable). The languages guarantee syntax and intent, not understanding.
